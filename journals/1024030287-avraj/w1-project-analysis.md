# Week 1: Project Analysis

## Objective

To understand the BloodLine project, its objectives, system requirements, and the overall problem of blood shortage prediction and donor management.

## Work Done

- Studied the BloodLine problem statement and project objectives.
- Understood the need for predicting future blood demand and identifying possible shortages.
- Studied the major modules of the system, including inventory management, demand forecasting, shortage-risk detection, donor matching, hospital requests, and reporting.
- Identified my primary contribution as the predictive component of the BloodLine system.
- Discussed how historical demand and current inventory can be used together for shortage analysis.

## My Contribution

My work was primarily focused on:

- Synthetic blood-demand data generation
- Demand forecasting
- Forecast evaluation
- Shortage-risk detection

## Key Learning

The forecasting module should not work independently. The predicted demand must be compared with current blood inventory so that the system can identify whether a shortage is likely.

## Outcome

The requirements and overall workflow for the demand forecasting and shortage-risk components were identified and understood.
